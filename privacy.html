Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What this app keeps

Your holiday notes, reviews, family names, quotes and photos are saved in the browser storage on the device you use. They are not uploaded to an Arthur's Holiday Passport account. You can export a backup from the Family page; that file is stored wherever you choose to save it.

Location and Google Maps

The app asks for your location only after you tap Find me. If allowed, your coordinates are stored in this browser and sent with your search to Google Maps Platform to find nearby places. If you type a town instead, that text is sent with the search. Google processes this information under its Privacy Policy.

Keeping your information

Your browser keeps locally saved app entries until you delete browser data or remove them in the app. Google Maps search results are shown for the current search. For a venue you choose from Google Maps, the app stores its place ID and your category; it looks up the venue name and address again when needed rather than keeping Google's search details in browser storage.

Contact

This is a family project. For questions about this policy, contact the family member who maintains the app.
